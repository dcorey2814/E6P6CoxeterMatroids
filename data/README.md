# Data

Lines are numbered 1..27 in the order a1..a6, b1..b6, c12, c13, ..., c56.

| file | content | written by |
|---|---|---|
| `subset_orbit_reps.dat` | the 5484 W(E6)-orbits of subsets of the 27 lines of sizes 1..26, one lexicographically minimal representative per line, sorted by size | `scripts/classify.jl` |
| `matroid_orbit_reps.dat` | the 230 W(E6)-orbits of (E6,P6)-matroids, in the same format; number 139 is the non-realizable orbit, number 230 is the set of all lines | `scripts/classify.jl` |
| `strata.csv` | for each of the 230 orbits: size, dimension of conv(Q), dimension of the stratum (-1 if empty), number of equations in the chosen chart, nonempty, nonempty over every prime | `scripts/strata_table.jl` |
| `gfan/cayley_plane_init.dat` | input of `gfan_initialforms`: the ideal of E6/P6 in the variables x01..x27 and the starting weight | `scripts/gfan_input.jl` |
| `gfan/symmetries.dat` | the six generators of W(E6) as permutations (0-based) with signs | `scripts/gfan_input.jl` |
| `gfan/cayley_plane_cone.dat` | input of `gfan_tropicaltraverse`: the starting cone and the symmetries | `scripts/gfan_traverse.sh` |
| `gfan/cayley_plane_trop.dat` | the tropical variety up to symmetry (max convention): 12885 rays, 14155 orbits of cones, 421 orbits of maximal cones | `scripts/gfan_traverse.sh` |
| `toric_degenerations.csv` | for each of the 60 prime orbits of maximal cones (numbered as in `MAXIMAL_CONES_ORBITS`, starting from 1): facets, lattice points, normalized volume, normality, h*-vector, whether the initial ideal is generated by quadrics, `order`/`chain` if the polytope is the order or chain polytope of the minuscule poset, f-vector | `scripts/toric_degenerations.jl` |
| `binomial_initial_ideals.txt` | one line `index\|lattice saturated\|rank\|saturated with respect to the variables` for each of the 388 orbits of maximal cones whose initial ideal is binomial (0-based index in `MAXIMAL_CONES_ORBITS`); the 60 with `true` in the last field are the prime orbits | Macaulay2, for the abstract |
| `toric_summary.json` | for each of the 60 prime orbits, keyed by its 0-based index: facets, lattice points, size of the Hilbert basis, normalized volume, normality, h*-vector, degrees of the generators of the initial ideal as `[degree, count]` pairs, and f-vector of the toric polytope | Macaulay2 and Normaliz, for the abstract |
