Coxeter matroids and their strata

A set Q of the 27 lines on a cubic surface is an (E₆,P₆)-matroid when it has the tritangent exchange property. Its stratum MQ is the set of points of E₆/P₆ whose nonzero coordinates are exactly the lines of Q.

The tropical E₆-Grassmannian

The tropicalization of the open stratum is a simplicial fan with a 6-dimensional lineality space Λ. Every cone induces a subdivision of the Gosset polytope 221 into matroid polytopes. Dimensions below are taken modulo Λ.

Toric degenerations

Definitions and where the numbers come from
Orbit
A W(E₆)-orbit of subsets of the 27 lines. The number of matroids in each orbit was found by applying every element of W(E₆), generated by the six symmetries recorded in the gfan file.
Qnr
Orbit —, of matroids with — lines, is the only orbit whose stratum is empty over every field. Every other stratum has a point over the algebraic closure of every prime field.
Stratum dimension
dim MQ, read off in a chart that admits a leaf order.
Charts and leaf orders
The explorer uses the chart y that the repository's good_chart picks: a line of Q with a leaf order and as few equations as possible. The signs of the equations come from the cubic D = aTCb + Pf(C).
Tropical dimensions
Cones of the tropical E₆-Grassmannian are counted modulo the lineality space Λ, so rays have dimension 1 and maximal cones dimension 10.
Subdivisions
The subdivision of 221 induced by a cone is the regular subdivision for the heights w, the sum of its rays, taken on the upper hull to match the max convention. Each cell is checked exactly, and the cells are identified with orbits of matroids. Two maximal cells are adjacent in the dual graph when they share a facet, that is, their common vertices span a 5-dimensional face. In the symmetric layout each cell is drawn at the barycenter of its vertices, projected to a plane in the span of 221 that the stabilizer of the cone preserves: a 2-dimensional irreducible piece of its action, or two 1-dimensional ones, or the rotation plane of one of its elements, chosen to keep at least half of the cells apart, the picture not too flat, and as much symmetry as possible. Cells that still land on the same point are spread on a small circle.
Prime orbits
Orbits of maximal cones whose initial ideal is binomial, with a saturated lattice, and saturated with respect to the variables. The order polytope gives the Hibi degeneration; the chain polytope is Stanley's chain polytope of the minuscule E₆ poset.
Data
The page reads nine files: matroid_orbits.csv, tropical_fvector.csv, ray_orbits.csv, maximal_cone_orbits.csv, toric_degenerations.csv, matroids.json, cones.csv, rays.csv and subdivisions.json with the files in data/subdivisions/. scripts/dashboard_data.py writes them from the files in data/ of the repository; docs/data/README.md describes every column.
